GCPC Wings

Research Wing

Back

Academic writing, literature review, and structural research methodologies that help members write, publish, and present high-quality scientific papers.

Wing Focus Areas

Research Methodologies

Building practical foundations in scientific research design, data collection, and qualitative/quantitative analysis.

Literature Review & Synthesis

Learning how to efficiently read, analyze, and synthesize academic literature to identify key research gaps.

Academic Writing & Publication

Mastering structure, academic tone, citations, and the formatting requirements of top-tier conferences and journals.

Key Activities

Literature Review Sessions

Regular group meetings where members analyze pioneering research papers and discuss innovative findings.

Technical Writing Bootcamps

Hands-on intensive bootcamps focused on writing structured abstracts, introductions, and methodologies.

Journal Submission Guides

Step-by-step guidance on understanding review processes, journal criteria, and responding to peer reviews.

Data Analytics Workshops

Guided sessions on data visualization tools, statistical software, and analysis models used in scientific projects.

How to Join

Requirements

  • Intellectual curiosity and strong analytical skills
  • Willingness to read scientific papers and academic literature
  • Commitment to structured academic writing
  • Consistent participation in research group discussions

Application Process

  • Register interest in the Research Wing through GCPC portal
  • Attend the Research Orientation & Topic Selection session
  • Complete an introductory literature summary exercise
  • Complete wing-specific research team onboarding
