GCPC Wings

Career & PR Wing

Back

Organizing career grooming bootcamps, communication workshops, mock interviews, branding support, and networking initiatives for professional readiness.

Wing Focus Areas

Professional Branding & CV

Crafting compelling LinkedIn profiles, engineering resumes, and digital portfolios to stand out to global recruiters.

Interview Prep & soft skills

Fostering verbal communication, corporate etiquette, body language, and structured behavioral interview tactics.

Public Relations & Outreach

Cultivating relationships with tech industry experts, tracking achievements, and promoting core club achievements.

Key Activities

Career Grooming Bootcamps

Multi-week comprehensive bootcamps involving professional recruiters, panels, and live simulated technical rounds.

CV & Resume Clinics

Individual review sessions with engineering managers to refine students' CVs for competitive internships.

Alumni Networking Panels

Interactive fireside chats and networking sessions featuring GCPC alumni working in lead tech roles.

Industry Study Visits

Organized field tours to leading software development companies to help members observe workplace environments.

How to Join

Requirements

  • Keen interest in professional networking, communication, and leadership
  • Willingness to engage in public relations, content creation, or event management
  • Consistent attendance in professional grooming and corporate sessions
  • Excellent collaboration, presentation, and teamwork abilities

Application Process

  • Register interest in the Career & PR Wing through GCPC membership portal
  • Participate in the Career Wing onboarding and orientation seminar
  • Participate in an informal communication skills review round
  • Complete a small mock pitch or collaborative task assignment
